# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 4, 2026

## Introduction

Social Media AI Agent ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Social Media AI Agent service. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

## Data We Collect

We collect and process the following data:
- Facebook/Instagram page access tokens (provided by you)
- Page content data (posts, comments, engagement metrics)
- User preferences and settings
- API usage logs for security and analytics

## How We Use Your Data

- To manage your social media pages
- To analyze content performance
- To generate AI-powered insights and recommendations
- To improve our service

## Data Security

We store access tokens securely in our database with industry-standard encryption. We do not share your data with third parties except Meta (Facebook/Instagram) as required for the service to function.

## User Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will delete:
- Your stored access tokens
- Your page data
- Your analysis history
